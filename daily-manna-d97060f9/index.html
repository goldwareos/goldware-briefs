1Where they are today

Checked on their live site and channels, Nov 12, 2026.

  • The ministry: a daily "One Year Bible" study taught by Pastor Bob Boone, a retired Calvary Chapel pastor from Bonners Ferry, Idaho. It airs live Monday to Friday at 8:30 AM Pacific, and Ladies' Daily Manna with Cheryl airs the 1st and 3rd Saturdays. They're now in season 7. They're a stand-alone 501(c)(3) para-church ministry as of January 2025.12
  • Website: a 6-page Wix site (Home, About, Live Stream, Daily Schedule, Donate, Contact). "Live Stream" sends people off-site to sermon.net, "Daily Schedule" is a PDF, and "Contact" is just a link to another link.1
  • Giving: a Wix donation form (one-time or monthly). 10% of gifts go to missionaries (Calvary Chapel Omsk, Russia, and Truth In Love Ministries).1
  • Audience: YouTube @CalvaryDailyManna has 350 subscribers and 493 videos, sorted into monthly playlists. They also stream on Facebook and sermon.net.34
  • The gap: the followers live on YouTube, Facebook and sermon.net, and the ministry owns no list of them. Followers can't easily talk with Bob, Cheryl or each other in one place, and reaching everyone today means texting or posting on three platforms.
A note on the word "podcast": they don't have an audio podcast feed today. It's a daily livestream. An Apple/Spotify audio feed is an easy add-on later (we're piloting the same thing for RCC).

3How this helps Daily Manna

Use these points when pitching it to Pastor Bob and Cheryl.

Reach everyone with one post, no texting
One post in the announcement channel, like "We're live in 5 minutes" or "No study Friday", reaches every member by in-app and email notification. The @everyone tag pings the whole group.68 There are no texting costs and no group-text "reply all" mess.
The followers become theirs
Today YouTube and Facebook decide who sees a stream. Every community member and every donor becomes a contact the ministry owns, with an email address, so they can reach their people even if a platform changes.
A place to talk about today's reading
Set up a "Today's Reading" channel for questions and comments on each day's passage, plus a Prayer Requests channel. Bob answers once and everyone sees it, and followers encourage each other. This fits what their own About page says: they exist to keep people "plugged into a daily devotional life" across long distances.2
Ladies' Daily Manna gets its own room
A private group or channel for Cheryl's Saturday ladies, where only Cheryl can make announcements.
The One Year Bible as a "course"
HighLevel puts courses inside communities.7 Each day's video and reading becomes a lesson, so someone who joins in March can start on Day 1 and keep track of their progress. That turns 493 videos into a guided path instead of a pile.
Live from inside the community
Go Live runs live video, audio and chat inside the community9. It works for a Q&A after the study or a members-only Ladies' session. They can keep streaming the daily study to YouTube and Facebook as they do now.
Easier giving that's tracked
Give on the site with one-time or monthly gifts through Stripe, and every donor saved in the CRM. A thank-you email goes out automatically, and they can follow up with donors, which a Wix form doesn't do for them.
Social handled, and Bob keeps teaching
Bob is a retired pastor. He should teach, not manage three platforms. With Care, we post, file each day's video into its month playlist, and draft comment replies. Bob approves the replies on one page.

6How we build it (step by step)

About 3–4 weeks from yes to launch.

  1. Discovery call (30 min)Jason
    Confirm: free community for everyone or a members tier; open discussion or mostly announcements; who answers comments (Bob, Cheryl, us); which social accounts (the Facebook is a profile link today, so check whether they have a Page); where the domain is registered; who owns the Stripe account for giving.
  2. Agreement and paymentJason
    A payment link for the setup and a recurring link for the plan (Commerce › Payment Links in the Goldware LLC sub-account).
  3. Create their sub-accountJake
    Check the sub-account cap first. Add Bob (and Cheryl) as Account users so they log in at app.goldware.io. Confirm Communities is turned on for the sub-account (Memberships › Communities).
  4. Collect contentJason
    Take the text from their current site verbatim, plus their logo and photos, the reading-plan PDF, the YouTube channel and playlists, and the giving wording (including the 10% missions line). Don't make up any facts.
  5. Build the website as a draftJake
    Build in HighLevel (AI Studio or classic Sites). Embed the YouTube live and playlist with a plain iframe (raw script tags don't run in AI Studio). Add the reading plan as a page, a giving page with Stripe, a contact form going to the CRM, and the Join button. Test on a phone before showing them.
  6. Build the communityJake
    Create the group "Daily Manna Family" with these channels: Announcements (owners only), Today's Reading, Prayer Requests, Ladies' Daily Manna, Introduce Yourself. Turn on email notifications. Add the One Year Bible course with the first month of lessons. Create a workflow so the site join form tags the contact, adds them to the group and sends a welcome email.
  7. Set up socialJason
    Platform: connect their accounts in Social Planner and show them how to use it. Care: have them add us as an Editor in YouTube Studio and as a Partner in Meta Business Suite. Never use their passwords.
  8. Review with Bob and CherylJason
    Walk them through the draft site and the community, collect changes, and do the 30-minute training: how to post an announcement, @everyone, Go Live, and approving replies.
  9. LaunchJake
    Point the domain to HighLevel, test every page and form live, and do a test gift. Keep Wix until a week after launch, then they cancel it.
  10. Bring the followers in (no texting)Bob + us
    Bob says it on the stream for a week: "Join us at ccdailymanna.org/join". We add a pinned YouTube comment, a link in the stream description, a Facebook post and a YouTube community post. Every new member gets the welcome email.
  11. After launchJason
    Check in after 2 weeks, and send the monthly report on Care plans. Offer the branded app or Sermon Shorts once the community is active.

7Decisions for Jason and Jake

  1. Ministry discount: do we charge the $2,000 setup (website $1,500 + community $500) or the full $3,000?
  2. Which plan do we lead with: Platform + Social Care at $447 (my recommendation), or Platform at $97 with Care offered as an upgrade?
  3. Do we offer the pilot price ($199 Care for 2 months) or hold full price?
  4. Branded app now or later? I'd say later, once the community has members.
  5. Sub-account cap: are we under it, or does this push us to a higher HighLevel plan?
Risks: (1) The community only works if Bob and Cheryl post in it for the first month, so put that in the training. (2) Followers are mostly older, so keep joining to one link and an email, with no app required. (3) Sources disagree on whether Communities is on HighLevel's Starter plan618, so confirm in our account before quoting. (4) Prices for outside tools move, so re-check before putting them in front of the client.
