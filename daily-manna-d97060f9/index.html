01Where they are today

Checked on their live site and channels, Nov 12, 2026.

  • The ministry: a daily "One Year Bible" study taught by Pastor Bob Boone, a retired Calvary Chapel pastor from Bonners Ferry, Idaho. It airs live Monday to Friday at 8:30 AM Pacific, and Ladies' Daily Manna with Cheryl airs the 1st and 3rd Saturdays. They're in season 7, and have been a stand-alone 501(c)(3) para-church ministry since January 2025.12
  • Website: a 6-page Wix site (Home, About, Live Stream, Daily Schedule, Donate, Contact). "Live Stream" sends people off-site to sermon.net, "Daily Schedule" is a PDF, and "Contact" is a link to another link.1
  • Giving: a Wix donation form (one-time or monthly). 10% of gifts go to missionaries (Calvary Chapel Omsk, Russia, and Truth In Love Ministries).1
  • Audience: YouTube @CalvaryDailyManna has 350 subscribers and 493 videos, sorted into monthly playlists. They also stream on Facebook and sermon.net.34
  • The gap: the followers live on YouTube, Facebook and sermon.net, and the ministry owns no list of them. Followers can't easily talk with Bob, Cheryl or each other in one place, and reaching everyone means texting or posting on three platforms.
On the word "podcast": they don't have an audio podcast feed today; it's a daily livestream. An Apple/Spotify audio feed is an easy add-on later (we're piloting the same thing for RCC).

02The offer in one line

One home for Daily Manna: a new website, a private Daily Manna community where every follower gets one message instead of a text, online giving, and their YouTube and Facebook handled for them. All on the GoldWare platform, built and run by GoldWare.
1. Website redesign
Watch live on the site, find any day's study, the reading plan as a real page, giving, and a join button.
2. Daily Manna Family
A private community: channels, an announcement channel, live video, and the reading plan as a "course". No limit on members.
3. Social media
They post themselves from GoldWare's one-screen scheduler (included), or we run it with our Care plan: posting, comment replies with Bob's approval, and a monthly report.

03How this helps Daily Manna

Use these points when pitching it to Pastor Bob and Cheryl.

Reach everyone with one post, no texting
One post in the announcement channel, like "We're live in 5 minutes" or "No study Friday", reaches every member by email and in-app notification. The @everyone tag pings the whole group. No texting costs and no "reply all" mess.
The followers become theirs
Today YouTube and Facebook decide who sees a stream. Every member and donor becomes a contact the ministry owns, so they can reach their people even if a platform changes.
A place to talk about today's reading
A "Today's Reading" channel for questions on each day's passage, plus Prayer Requests. Bob answers once and everyone sees it. It fits their own About page: keeping people "plugged into a daily devotional life" across long distances.2
Ladies' Daily Manna gets its own room
A private channel for Cheryl's Saturday ladies, where only Cheryl makes announcements.
The One Year Bible as a "course"
Each day's video and reading becomes a lesson, so someone who joins in March can start on Day 1 and keep their place. 493 videos become a guided path instead of a pile.
Live from inside the community
Live video, audio and chat inside the group, for a Q&A after the study or a members-only Ladies' session. The daily study keeps streaming to YouTube and Facebook as now.
Easier giving that's tracked
One-time or monthly gifts on the site through Stripe, every donor saved, and an automatic thank-you email. A Wix form doesn't do that for them.
Social handled, and Bob keeps teaching
Bob should teach, not manage three platforms. With Care, we post, file each day's video into its month playlist, and draft comment replies Bob approves on one page.

04How to sell it

One 30–45 minute conversation with Bob and Cheryl, ideally in person or on a video call with a phone in hand. Order: website → group messaging → social media, each leading into the next. Jason leads (pastor to pastor); Jake runs the demos.

Before the call (about 1 hour, Jake):
  • Their current site open on a phone, ready to show.
  • The slide deck (Dropbox › Goldware › Daily Manna).
  • A demo community in our own GoldWare account, "Daily Manna Family (demo)", with Announcements, Today's Reading, Prayer Requests and Ladies' Daily Manna. Add Jason's phone and email as a member so an @everyone post visibly arrives during the call.
  • The social scheduler open with a week of sample posts.

Open with their mission, not our product (2 min)

Say: "Your goal is to keep people in God's Word every day, across a lot of miles. Everything we'd suggest is about helping more people do that with less work for you two."

Ask: "What's the hardest part of running Daily Manna right now?" Let them talk, and tie the rest back to it.

Step 1 · The website, the front door (10 min)

Ask: "When someone hears about Daily Manna, where do you send them? What happens when they get there?"

Show them on the phone:

  • "Live Stream" takes people off the site to sermon.net.
  • The reading schedule is a PDF, hard to read on a phone.
  • Contact is a link to another link.
  • No way to join or stay connected. People watch, then leave.

Say: "A ministry website should let people watch today's study, find any day, and join and give. Right now it mostly tells people where else to go."

Show: the mockup: Watch, Reading Plan, Give, and the big Join the Daily Manna Family button.

Price: "The redesign is normally $2,000. For the ministry it's $1,500."

Bridge: "That Join button is the important part. Once someone joins, how do you stay in touch with them?"

Step 2 · Group messaging, what they asked for (15 min)

Ask: "How do you reach everyone today, say when the stream moves or you're sick? Can they talk to each other?"

Say: "Imagine one private place that's just Daily Manna. You post once and everyone gets it. Nobody has to text anyone."

Demo (this sells it):

  • Post "@everyone We're live in 5 minutes!" and let them see Jason's phone light up.
  • Today's Reading: Bob answers once and everyone sees it.
  • Prayer Requests, and Ladies' Daily Manna as Cheryl's own room.
  • The One Year Bible course: join in March, start on Day 1.
  • Go Live for a members-only Q&A after the study.

Line to land: "Right now your followers belong to YouTube and Facebook. This way they belong to Daily Manna."

Price: "Setup is $500: building it, the join page, a welcome email, and a launch script Bob reads on the stream. After that it's part of the $97 a month, which also covers the website and giving. No per-member cost."

"Our people are older and not techy."
"They tap one link and use their email; no app required. Bob mentions it on the stream for a week, and a welcome email walks them through it."
"We already have Facebook."
"Keep it, we'll still post there. But Facebook decides who sees your posts, and you don't own that list. The community is yours: no ads, no distractions."
"Will it be a lot of work?"
"About 5 minutes a day: one announcement and a couple of replies. We set it all up and help you get it going the first month."
"Can we just text people?"
"Every text costs money, needs carrier registration, and people can't talk to each other. This replaces the group text."

Step 3 · Social media post control (8 min)

Ask: "Who posts to YouTube and Facebook today? How long does it take? Who answers comments?"

A. You control it
Included in the $97 plan. One screen, a week of posts scheduled to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram at once, and a calendar of what's going out.
Internal: the scheduler can't file videos into YouTube playlists, so playlists stay manual on this option.
B. We do it, you approve
Social Care. We post, title and file every stream into its month playlist, and draft replies Bob approves on one page; pastoral comments go to him. Monthly report. Partner access, never their passwords.

Recommend B if Bob or Cheryl post themselves today: "Bob, your time is best spent teaching. Let us carry the rest."

Price: "Care is $397 a month alone. With the website and community it's $447 a month for everything. We can start with two months at $199 so you can see it work."

Close (5 min)

Recap: "A website that lets people watch, read and join, a community where you reach everyone with one post, and your social handled."

Show the three plans ($97 / $447 / $647) and point at the middle one. Setup is $2,000 with ministry pricing.

Ask: "Would you like us to get started? We'd have it live in about four weeks."

If yes: agreement and payment link today, a 30-minute kickoff this week, and Bob announces the community on the stream the week we launch.

07How we build it

About 4 weeks from yes to launch.

  1. Discovery call (30 min)Jason
    Confirm: free community or a members tier; open discussion or mostly announcements; who answers comments; which social accounts (their Facebook link is a profile, so check for a Page); where the domain is registered; who owns the Stripe account.
  2. Agreement and paymentJason
    A payment link for the setup and a recurring link for the plan, from GoldWare billing.
  3. Create their client accountJake
    Check our account limit first. Add Bob (and Cheryl) as users so they log in at app.goldware.io. Turn on Communities for the account.
  4. Collect contentJason
    Their site text verbatim, logo and photos, the reading-plan PDF, the YouTube channel and playlists, and the giving wording (with the 10% missions line). Don't make up any facts.
  5. Build the website as a draftJake
    Embed the YouTube live and playlists with a plain iframe (raw script tags don't run in the AI site builder). Reading plan page, Stripe giving page, contact form into their contacts, Join button. Test on a phone first.
  6. Build the Daily Manna FamilyJake
    Group with Announcements (owners only), Today's Reading, Prayer Requests, Ladies' Daily Manna, Introduce Yourself. Email notifications on. One Year Bible course, first month loaded. A workflow: join form → tag → add to group → welcome email.
  7. Set up socialJason
    Home Base: connect their accounts to the scheduler and show them how to use it. Care: they add us as an Editor in YouTube Studio and a Partner in Meta Business Suite. Never their passwords.
  8. Review with Bob and CherylJason
    Walk through the draft site and community, collect changes, and a 30-minute training: posting an announcement, @everyone, Go Live, approving replies.
  9. LaunchJake
    Point the domain to the new site, test every page and form live, do a test gift. Keep Wix a week after launch, then they cancel it.
  10. Bring the followers in (no texting)Bob + us
    Bob says it on the stream for a week: "Join us at ccdailymanna.org/join". Pinned YouTube comment, stream description link, Facebook post, YouTube community post. Every new member gets the welcome email.
  11. After launchJason
    Check in after 2 weeks; monthly report on Care plans. Offer the app or Sermon Shorts once the community is active.

08Decisions for Jason & Jake

  1. Ministry discount: $2,000 setup (website $1,500 + community $500) or the full $3,000?
  2. Lead with Home Base + Social Care at $447 (recommended), or Home Base at $97 with Care as an upgrade?
  3. Offer the pilot price ($199 Care for 2 months) or hold full price?
  4. Mobile app now or later? Later, once the community has members.
  5. Account limit: are we under it, or does this push our platform plan up?
Risks: (1) The community only works if Bob and Cheryl post in it the first month, so build that into the training. (2) Followers are mostly older: keep joining to one link and an email, no app required. (3) Confirm Communities is on our platform plan before quoting. (4) Outside tool prices move; re-check before showing the client.
