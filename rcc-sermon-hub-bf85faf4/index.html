Can GoldWare do what Subsplash does for a church?

A sermon manager, an organized message archive with video and audio, on the church website and in a church app. We'd test it on our own church first, next to Subsplash, before offering it to anyone else. This is a separate idea from the Daily Manna proposal.

01The question

Short answer: mostly yes. The GoldWare platform covers the website, the video and audio pages, the app, and online giving on the website. It has no ready-made "sermon" record and no podcast hosting, so we fill those two pieces ourselves. A few Subsplash features we wouldn't match at all: giving inside the app, a no-login app, and text-to-give.

Before we show it to anyone else, we prove it on RCC. That's our own church, it already uses Subsplash, and our message videos and audio are already produced every week.

02What RCC uses Subsplash for today

Checked Nov 12, 2026.

  • Church app: subsplash.com/regencc/app (both app stores).1
  • Giving: subsplash.com/regencc/give.2

05The angle

Don't sell it as "Subsplash for less." Subsplash and Nucleus are polished products, and a cheaper copy means we maintain the media piece forever. Sell it as done for you: one GoldWare system for website, message archive, podcast, app and social, and we post each week's message for them. Our Sermon Shorts pipeline already produces the video and audio, so the weekly upload is work we're half doing anyway.

06The test on RCC

Rule: nothing live changes. regencc.com, the RCC app and Subsplash stay exactly as they are until you two decide. The test is built as its own project in the RCC account and reviewed on a private preview link.
  1. Message library: the Book of Ruth firstAll 4 Wednesday messages already have edited videos and audio (table below). Then one Sunday series (2 Corinthians) to show a long series.
  2. Archive pagesA series page and a page per message: video, audio player, passage, date, description. Browse by series, speaker, book.
  3. PodcastThe Ruth feed we already host, linked from each message ("Listen on Apple / Spotify").
  4. Sermon managerTest the two options (blog posts vs. a sermon list) for how long a weekly upload takes, and pick one.
  5. App (optional, last)The branded app with series as courses, on test phones only, never published.
  6. Side-by-side reviewOne page for Jason and Jake: what matched Subsplash, what didn't, minutes per weekly upload, monthly cost.
